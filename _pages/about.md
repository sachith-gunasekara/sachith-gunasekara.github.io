Hi! I'm Sachith, a Senior AI Engineer at [Gapstars](https://gapstars.net), working with Envision AI Labs in Leiden, Netherlands. Most of my days revolve around one question: how do we know an AI system is actually good? I build retrieval benchmarks and evaluation frameworks for RAG and agentic systems, and I make sure every insight we generate from pharmaceutical and clinical data can be traced back to its source. Before this, I built multi-agent systems at Arcadea Group and [Surge Global](https://surge.global), after graduating at the top of my class (First Class, ranked 1st of 125) in Computer Science from the [University of Kelaniya](https://kln.ac.lk/).

On the research side, I'm fascinated by how language models reason, and how we can help them reason better without simply throwing more compute at the problem. My latest work, iSelf-Discover, asks a deceptively simple question: should a model plan its reasoning in structured JSON or in plain language? Plain language won, by up to 18.9% on MATH. Before that, I helped build OpenBezoar, a recipe for training small, open instruction-following models on a modest budget.

I love sharing what I learn. I teach on StemLink's AI Engineering Bootcamp, where I get to watch learners go from their first transformer to working multi-agent demos. I've also given talks such as [A Step Beyond the Hype](https://youtu.be/GbkTn_7Xyr8?t=419) and [Implementing AI Agents](https://youtu.be/4oJvr5uVTTg?t=256), and I write the occasional deep dive, like [nanoJAXGPT](https://huggingface.co/blog/sachithgunasekara/nanojaxgpt), a hands-on introduction to JAX/Equinox. Looking ahead, I'm keen to pursue a PhD in Computer Science focused on making LLMs reason better and more efficiently. If that overlaps with what you're working on, I'd love to chat!
